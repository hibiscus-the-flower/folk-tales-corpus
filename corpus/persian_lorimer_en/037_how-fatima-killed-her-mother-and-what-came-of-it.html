Fatima went home to her mother as she had been told, and said : " Mother, I want a pomegranate." " Our shepherd is coming back, he will pick it for you, my child," said her mother. — " No, I want it from your own hand." " Come along, then," said her mother, " and I'll get one for you." They went off together and came into the garden, and Fatima pointed to a very big pomegranate and said : " I want that one." Her mother climbed up to the very top of the tree to get it for her, when the girl suddenly cried out : " Mother, your youngest brother's dead ! " The moment she heard this, the mother threw herself down and was killed.

Fatima's father arrived on the scene a little later and saw that his wife had fallen down and been killed, and they carried her away and buried her.
