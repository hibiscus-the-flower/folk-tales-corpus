The Honor Due to Whatever is Truly Useful

Rabbi Huna once asked his son Raba, why he did not attend the lectures of Rabbi Hisda. "Because," replied the son, "he only treats of temporal and worldly concerns." "What," said the father, "he occupies himself with that which is necessary for the preservation of human beings—and this you call worldly affairs! Trust me, this is among the most estimable of studies."

Shabbat, 82a.
