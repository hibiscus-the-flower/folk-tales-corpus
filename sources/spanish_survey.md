## COLONIAL-SOURCE NOTES
No colonial concerns. All collectors working within Iberian tradition or as translators of native Spanish material. The Eells/Sellers "Nuts & Grapes" includes some Latin American (Araucanian/Araucania) content — those sections were auto-skipped (empty bodies) and are NOT in the corpus, which is appropriate given the colonial-framing risk of 19th-century European accounts of indigenous South American peoples.
